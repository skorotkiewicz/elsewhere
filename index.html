Elsewhere is a small escape: real recorded journeys, a window seat, and a soundscape made just for you. No destination required.

Make it your own

Board to enable audio. Shape the engine, rain, and passenger sounds with the mixer. The soundscape is synthesized in your browser; the video itself stays muted. Local-language voices depend on those installed on your device.

Announcements and stops are imaginative, not a factual guide to the recorded route. Day mode changes the tint, not the time in the original footage.

The windows

    Window photographs: Unsplash. Video availability and embedding are controlled by their creators and YouTube. If footage can’t load, the still window and soundscape remain available.

    For a smooth ride

    Run the Bun server over localhost or HTTPS; YouTube may block file:// embeds. YouTube receives connection and playback data when its player loads. Keyboard: Space to pause, M to mute, F for fullscreen. Escape closes a popup.
